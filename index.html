SoCal Sky Now — live airport weather

About this map. Every dot is an airport weather station reporting a METAR observation, coloured by its current flight category (VFR = clear flying weather, LIFR = fog or very low cloud). Click a station to see live temperature, wind, visibility and sky condition. The data comes straight from the NOAA METAR current weather feed in ArcGIS Living Atlas, a live feature service that NOAA and Esri refresh about once an hour, so the map changes on its own. I started from Esri's Intro to FeatureLayer sample and changed the dataset, the basemap, the centre and zoom (Southern California), the renderer (flight category colours), the popup, and added a Legend and a Search box. Zoom out to see every reporting airport in the world.
